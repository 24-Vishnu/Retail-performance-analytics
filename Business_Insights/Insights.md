**Business insights**

The purpose of this step is to convert our Power BI charts into meaningful business conclusions.

We will use the dashboard we created.

    -	Identify the Top Sales Region
          The West region generates the highest sales, while the South region has the lowest sales.
        
    -	Identify top-performing category 
          Technology is the highest-performing product category in terms of sales, while Office Supplies has the lowest sales among the three categories.
        
    -	Identify top-performing segment 
          The Consumer segment contributes the highest sales, indicating that individual consumers are the largest customer segment by sales.
        
    -	Analyze shipping preferences 
          Standard Class is the most commonly associated shipping mode with sales, while Same Day has the lowest sales contribution.
        
    -	Analyze overall Sales vs Profit 
          The business generated approximately 2.30 million in sales and 286.40 thousand in profit across approximately 5,009 distinct orders.
